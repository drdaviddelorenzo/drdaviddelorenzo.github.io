Data Science

Genomics · Machine Learning · Global Health

I am a scientist with a multidisciplinary background in genomics and data science, passionate about understanding the interactions between genetics and the environment as they relate to human health.

With over 30 years of experience in population genetics, molecular diagnostics, and data analysis, I am currently a Senior Data Scientist at UCL London. My work focuses on optimising clinical decision support using advanced statistical and machine learning techniques in the context of the Neotree project — exploring the feasibility of AI-driven diagnostic algorithms in low-resource settings.

What I do

Genomics & Population Genetics

Over three decades studying how genetic variation shapes health outcomes across populations — from molecular diagnostics to large-scale genomic data analysis.

Machine Learning & Clinical AI

Developing and validating machine learning models for clinical decision support, with a focus on deployability in low- and middle-income country settings.

Global & One Health

Integrating human, animal, and environmental health perspectives. Interested in the interface between data science, epidemiology, and public health at a global scale.

Teaching & Mentoring

Teaching data science, genomics, and nutrigenomics to researchers and students. I also mentor early-career researchers through the in2research programme.
